Codex has changed my life as a solopreneur Codex 彻底改变了我作为个体创业者的生活

作者：Jonny Miller（@jonnym1ller） · 原文：https://x.com/jonnym1ller/status/2095646368165769285 · 中英双语对照 · 译文供个人阅读参考

It's difficult to exaggerate how absurdly life-changing Codex has been for me as a solopreneur in recent months. And yet people have been saying 'but what do you actually use it for' (so I decided to write this).

很难夸大 Codex 在最近几个月里对我这个个体创业者（solopreneur）的生活带来了多么离谱的改变。可还是不断有人问"那你到底拿它来干嘛"（所以我决定写这篇）。

I work a few hours each morning (mostly dictating to Codex). I would say 95% of the work I do now happens directly inside the Codex app.

我每天早上工作几个小时（大部分时间是对着 Codex 口述）。可以说，我现在 95% 的工作都直接在 Codex app 里完成。

A few months back, I replaced Collective, which was costing ~$300 a month despite frankly antiquated software. Codex now handles all of my S-Corp accounting and tax filing with recurring scheduled tasks. These savings alone is more than covering my monthly token spend.

几个月前，我把 Collective 换掉了——它每月要花我大约 300 美元，软件却老掉牙。现在 Codex 用周期性的定时任务（recurring scheduled tasks）打理我 S-Corp 的全部记账和报税。光这一项省下的钱，就已经不止覆盖我每月的 token 开销了。

Then I replaced the ScoreApp ($150 a month) assessment tool with this better (in my opinion) custom assessment tool that generates a highly personalised results page and 30 day training plan (which generated 1500 new leads in the past month)

接着我把 ScoreApp（每月 150 美元）的测评工具也换成了这个（我自认为）更好的定制测评工具，它能生成高度个性化的结果页和 30 天训练计划（过去一个月带来了 1500 条新线索）。

Then I rebuilt the Stateshift app entirely myself with no prior design or dev experience (I'm especially stoked with this, retention is up and the app is currently generating $6k/mo in revenue and more importantly bringing in hundreds of new leads)

然后我在完全没有设计和开发经验的情况下，自己把 Stateshift app 整个重建了一遍（这个我尤其得意，留存率上去了，这个 app 目前每月带来 6000 美元收入，更重要的是引来了数百条新线索）。

Codex also helped create a design system, KPI dashboard and rebuilt the Nervous System Mastery homepage to replace the old Webflow site (application CTR have also increased) and Thrivecart checkout page.

Codex 还帮我做了一套设计系统（design system）、一个 KPI 仪表盘，并重建了 Nervous System Mastery 的主页来替换掉老的 Webflow 站点（申请页的点击率 CTR 也上升了），还有 Thrivecart 结账页。

Then I had the idea to review student posts from our circle community and asked for permission from the students and then created this incredible outcomes page (which would have taken hundreds of hours to manually create)

后来我想到去梳理我们 circle 社群里学员发的帖子，征得学员同意后，做出了这个超赞的成果展示页（outcomes page）（要是手工做，得花上几百个小时）。

Codex also provides high-quality editorial feedback directly in Google Docs on the new essay I'm writing (although currently I still find it worthwhile to hire a human editor for the final 15%) and built a custom website to host the essay.

Codex 还会直接在 Google Docs 里，对我正在写的新长文给出高质量的编辑意见（不过眼下我仍觉得最后 15% 值得请一位人类编辑来做），并搭了一个定制网站来托管这篇文章。

It also replaced the $200 per video podcast episode I was paying a freelancer, and is able to edit 3 camera video interviews with, sync 2 hours of audio and upload to Youtube with show notes and timestamps (final edit here).

它还顶替了我原本每期要付给自由职业者 200 美元的视频播客剪辑——它能剪 3 机位的视频访谈、同步 2 小时的音频，并连同节目简介和时间戳一起上传到 Youtube（成片在这里）。

And this morning it built an Instagram carousel creation engine.

就在今天早上，它还搭了一个 Instagram 轮播图（carousel）生成引擎。

It pulls 80+ transcripts from podcast appearances on YouTube and created screen grabs of me talking enthusiastically.

它从 YouTube 上我参加过的播客里抓取了 80 多份文字稿，并截取了我兴致勃勃讲话的画面。

Codex then generates two months' worth of Instagram carousels with captions based on the transcripts, connects to ManyChat and passes on for me to review before scheduling directly to Instagram.

然后 Codex 基于这些文字稿生成够用两个月的 Instagram 轮播图，配好文案，接入 ManyChat，交给我审核，之后直接排期发布到 Instagram。

(for context, I hadn't posted anything to Instagram for months because I didn't have the time).

（交代一下背景：我已经好几个月没往 Instagram 发过任何东西了，因为实在没时间。）

There are definitely some tricks to be learned along the way, stuff it doesn't quite get first time and I still reply to my own emails + handwrite any public-facing copy myself (AI writing isn't quite there yet).

这一路上确实有些门道要摸索，有些东西它第一次做不太到位，而且我依然自己回自己的邮件、自己手写所有对外的文案（AI 写作还没到那个火候）。

But in general it's difficult to imagine what life was like pre-AGI and I certainly can't imagine a better time to be working as a small business owner with big dreams!

但总的来说，已经很难想象前 AGI 时代的生活是什么样了，而且我实在想不出，还有什么比现在更适合一个怀揣大梦想的小企业主去打拼的时代！
